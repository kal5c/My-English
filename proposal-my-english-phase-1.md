## Project goal

Create a polished, responsive website that gives My English a clear home on the web, helps visitors move from social content to a useful next step, and presents the school’s learning options professionally. The site will connect the audience already following My English on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram with course information, free lessons and direct contact options.

The website is intended to make it easier for prospective learners to understand the available options and contact the school. It is not a guarantee of a particular increase in followers, inquiries or student enrollments.

## Phase 1 — Front-end website

Phase 1 delivers the public-facing website as a working front-end demo. It includes responsive navigation and these pages:

- **Home:** My English introduction, online and in-person learning message, calls to explore programs or find a level, exam-preparation highlights, free-lesson previews and contact links.
- **Find your level:** A short, informal four-question English check with a progress indicator and a suggested conversation starter. It is not an official placement test and does not save answers.
- **Programs:** Ticket-inspired cards for **Online**, **Group** and **VIP** options. Fees, class size, schedules, course duration and other unspecified details will be confirmed by the school rather than invented.
- **Exam preparation:** Information pages for **IELTS**, **TOEFL**, **SAT** and **visa-interview practice**, with contact-based next steps.
- **Free lessons:** A topic-filtered library featuring selected public videos from the My English YouTube channel, plus a link to the teacher’s TikTok profile for short lessons.
- **About Yared:** An introduction based on the information supplied by My English, with social profile links and figures attributed to the supplied profile details.
- **Results:** A prepared area for real student stories and results, to be populated only with school-approved information and permission to publish.
- **FAQ and Contact:** Answers to common questions, school contact information and a course-interest form preview.

The site will use the supplied My English logo and the contact details provided: phone **0968901570** and Telegram **t.me/myEng_123**. It will link to the supplied YouTube, TikTok and Instagram profiles. English is the primary copy. The Amharic control will be shown, but Amharic page text should only be added after the school approves the translation.

The course-interest form is a front-end prototype. It prepares a message for the visitor to copy and send; it does not transmit or store form details.

## Business value of Phase 1

The website provides a destination for social followers who want to explore classes, understand the learning formats, try an informal level check, watch free lessons or contact the school. Clear program cards and inquiry actions reduce uncertainty for prospective learners. A topic-organized free lesson library helps visitors discover the teacher’s public learning content while keeping the original videos on their source platforms.

## Phase 1 exclusions

This phase does **not** include a working student account system, payment processing, registration or enrollment records, protected course access, a course-management system, student notifications, stored progress tracking, a teacher/admin dashboard, or automated form submission. The website must not present any of those functions as active.

## Optional Phase 2 — Separately scoped and priced upgrades

If My English wants to operate online courses directly through the website, a future upgrade can be scoped and priced separately. Potential features include:

- Student registration, sign-in and enrollment management.
- Payment integration using a payment method available to the school.
- Private course areas for recorded lessons, live-class links or both.
- Course and lesson management for the teacher.
- Progress tracking, quizzes and teacher feedback.
- Student notifications and class updates.
- An admin dashboard for managing students, programs, content and course activity.

The upgrade scope will depend on the school’s preferred payment provider, class-delivery method, content format, country/business requirements and administrative workflow. These capabilities are not included in the Phase 1 fee.

## Information needed from My English

Before publishing final client-facing copy, the school should confirm current program descriptions, course levels, delivery formats, schedules, start dates, prices, class sizes, duration, and the location/address for in-person learning.
